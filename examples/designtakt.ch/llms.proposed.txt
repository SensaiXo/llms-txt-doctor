# designtakt

> designtakt GmbH is a Swiss agency in Bern, Switzerland for websites, branding and SEO/AEO, founded by Juliane Rettich (Strategy & Design) and Patrick Hux (Development & SEO/AEO), with strategy, design and development in-house. It builds Webflow websites (Webflow Certified Partner since 2023) and web applications in Next.js, React and Supabase, plus branding and SEO/AEO, for startups, SMEs and corporates in the DACH region. Engagements start with a discovery call followed by a fixed-price proposal; the team has 8+ years of experience in design and development. All pages linked below are in German; this file is in English. (verify: total project count — the site currently states 60+, 50+ and 30+ in different places.)
