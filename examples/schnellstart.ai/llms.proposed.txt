# schnellstart.ai

> KI-Beratung for Swiss SMEs (KMU) by Lukas Huber (CEO) and Florian Witschi (CTO), Ringstrasse 37, 8500 Frauenfeld, Thurgau, CH; VAT CHE-163.272.208; info@schnellstart.ai; market Switzerland; founded 2024.

Offer, CHF excl. VAT: fixed-price KI-Standortbestimmung (5-Säulen-Readiness-Audit, use-case prioritisation, ROI-Fahrplan) after a free 15-minute Erstgespräch, team workshops at CHF 2'200 half day and CHF 3'800 full day, consulting from CHF 450/h, Enterprise subscriptions, and implementation projects (n8n, Make, Zapier) quoted after the Standortbestimmung.
Language: German-first (de, de-CH) with published English pages for booking, the Copilot guide and the Blueprint (en, en-CH).
Tools are chosen per case; open-source where possible (n8n, self-hosted), no vendor lock-in; Swiss business integrations Bexio, Swiss21, Sage, Abacus.
Swiss hosting (Infomaniak, Geneva) is offered where a case calls for it, while the industry pages state Swiss-hosted, FADP-compliant delivery (verify: which of the two applies by default for healthcare, legal and Treuhand).
Typical trigger situations: Microsoft 365 Copilot has appeared in a tenant and nobody has decided what it may read; staff paste company data into private ChatGPT accounts with no rule; several automation ideas exist with no way to rank them; a repetitive back-office process (invoices, receipts, quotes, scheduling, reporting) eats hours every week.
